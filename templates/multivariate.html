What is Multivariate Analysis?
Overview

Multivariate analysis reveals large-scale relationships across multiple polygenic scores and samples simultaneously, helping you discover patterns that aren't visible when looking at individual scores.

Visualization Options
Heatmaps
Overall data patterns and outliers across your entire dataset
UMAP & t-SNE
Hidden clusters of similar samples or groups of related traits
PCA
The main drivers of variation in your data
SVD Analysis
Fundamental data structure and component relationships
How to Use
  1. Select Data: Choose multiple PGS scores (5+ recommended for meaningful patterns)
  2. Choose Method: Pick your visualization method based on your research question
  3. Set Perspective: Decide between sample-centered or score-centered analysis
  4. Generate & Explore: Create your visualization and investigate the large-scale patterns
Configuration Options
Sample-Centered

Points represent individual samples. Shows which samples have similar polygenic risk profiles across multiple traits.

Score-Centered

Points represent PGS scores. Shows which polygenic scores tend to correlate and form groups of related traits.

Use Cases

Sample-centered analysis: Discover if samples cluster by ancestry, disease status, or other population characteristics.

Score-centered analysis: Find groups of related traits such as cardiovascular vs metabolic vs psychiatric scores.
